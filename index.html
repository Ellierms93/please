Built for proper camping

Facilities

In progress right now for the 2027 season.

Our toilet and shower block will include four unisex toilets and showers, a family bathroom with bath, shower, toilet, basin and baby change station, underfloor heating, an undercover washing-up area, washing machine and tumble dryer, community refrigerator, children's play area, petting farm and mini beach.

Make Your Stay Even More Special

There’s more to a stay at The Camp Inn than your pitch. Add farm entry when you book and enjoy a discounted guest rate, with bigger savings the more days you add, perfect if you want to keep popping back to see the fluffy ones throughout your stay.

Want to get even closer? Make your stay one to remember with one of our private animal experiences. Get up close with our Highland cows to groom, feed and cuddle them and, when appropriate, perhaps even head out for a halter walk. Or take our alpacas for a wander through the Lincolnshire countryside.

Whether it’s a quick farm visit or a proper hands-on experience, you can build a little more farm life into your stay.
